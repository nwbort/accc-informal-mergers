Sydney Transport Partners Consortium (including Transurban) - proposed acquisition of WestConnex interest

Status

Completed

Outcome

Not opposed subject to undertakings
on
30 August 2018

Reference number

63264

Industry

Toll Roads

Commenced

26 February 2018

Total review days

97 days

Acquirer(s)

  • Sydney Transport Partners Consortium (including Transurban)

Target(s)

  • WestConnex

Summary

Sydney Transport Partners Consortium (including Transurban) proposes to acquire a majority interest in WestConnex.

The ACCC accepted a s87B undertaking from Transurban in this matter. Transurban publishes traffic data pursuant to the undertaking at https://nswtollroaddata.com.

Timeline

Date Event
26 Feb 2018

ACCC commenced review under the Merger Process Guidelines.
19 Mar 2018

Closing date for submissions from interested parties.
6 Apr 2018

Former proposed decision date of 26 April delayed to allow provision of requested information from the Sydney Transport Partners Consortium.
20 Apr 2018

ACCC received further information from the Sydney Transport Partners Consortium.
17 May 2018

ACCC published a Statement of Issues outlining preliminary competition concerns.
31 May 2018

Closing date for submissions relating to Statement of Issues.
19 Jul 2018

Former decision date of 19 July delayed to allow more time to consider the competition issues and obtain further information from the parties and market participants.
9 Aug 2018

Draft 87B undertaking proffered by Transurban. ACCC commenced market inquiries.
21 Aug 2018

Former proposed decision date of 6 September 2018 brought forward to 30 August 2018 to take into account the NSW Government's timetable for the sale of WestConnex.
30 Aug 2018

ACCC announced it would not oppose the proposed acquisition, subject to section 87B undertakings accepted by the ACCC.

During the course of the review the ACCC also made a number of document and information requests to the parties that are not all separately identified in the entries above. Collectively, the parties took 29 business days to respond to these other requests.
