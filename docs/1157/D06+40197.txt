1. 
Background 
1 . l  
OneSteel and Smorgon each operate as steel manufacturers and distributors in Australia. 
The directors of OneSteel and Smorgon have agreed to propose a scheme of arrangement 
which if approved, will result in OneSteel acquiring all of the shares in Smorgon. 
1.2 
OneSteel has informed the Commission that it intends to divest Smorgon Distribution by 
way of demerger and OneSteel has offered to the Commission the section 878 
Undertakings set out in this document in order to make provision for the divestment of 
Smorgon Distribution. 
2. 
Commencement and Duration of Undertakings 
2.1 
Clauses 1, 2, 3, 4.1, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12 of this Undertaking come into effect when: 
(a) 
the Undertaking is executed by OneSteel; and 
(b) 
the Commission accepts the Undertaking so executed by OneSteel. 
2.2 
Clauses 4 (except clause 4.1) and 5 of this Undertaking come into effect on the 
Implementation Date. 
2.3 
This Undertaking will cease to have effect when the time period specified in clause 5.1 has 
expired or on the date of any announcement to the Australian Stock Exchange by 
OneSteel that it no longer intends to proceed with the schemes of arrangement to acquire 
all of the shares in Smorgon. 
3. 
Undertaking to divest Smorgon Distribution 
3.1 
Subject to clauses 3.5 and 3.6, OneSteel undertakes to the Commission that it will cease to 
have any ownership or control over Smorgon Distribution by the end of the Divestiture 
Period, following the demerger of Smorgon Distribution by way of scheme of arrangement. 
3.2 
Prior to the Implementation Date OneSteel must provide to the Commission : 
(a) 
a timetable for the demerger of Smorgon Distribution; 
(b) 
descriptions of the property, buildings, equipment, stock and other assets owned or 
used by Smorgon Distribution in the ordinary course of business; 
(c) 
descriptions of Smorgon Distribution's operations; and 
(d) 
a list of the Contracts relevant to Smorgon Distribution.

5. 
Anti-dumping applications 
5.1 
For three years from the Implementation Date, OneSteel and its related bodies corporate 
must not make an application, nor participate with any other person in making an 
application, nor procure or encourage any other person to make an application under Part 
XVB, Division 2 of the Customs Act 1901 in relation to the importation of merchant bar, 
reinforcing rod and bar, bar for grinding media, wire, structural pipe or RHS products into 
Australia by any person, unless: 
OneSteel has obtained from a reputable expert in the field who is approved by the 
Commission (the Adviser) an opinion to the effect that in the Adviser's view an 
application proposed to be made by OneSteel (or a group of persons including 
OneSteel) is bona fide and is not frivolous or vexatious and, based upon the 
information made available to the Adviser by OneSteel, in the Adviser's view there 
are reasonable grounds for the publication of a dumping duty notice or a 
countewailing duty notice in respect of the goods which are the subject of the 
application, and has provided this opinion on a confidential basis to the 
Commission at least one month before any application is made; 
following the provision to the Commission of an opinion in accordance with 
paragraph 5.1 (a), OneSteel has consulted in good faith and on a confidential basis 
with the Commission in relation to the proposed application and procured that the 
Adviser has consulted with the Commission and taken into account any views put 
forward by the Commission in relation to the proposed application; and 
following the consultation required by paragraph 5.l(b), the Adviser has provided 
to the Commission on a confidential basis a certificate to the effect that, based 
upon the information and views made available to the Adviser by OneSteel and the 
Commission, in the Adviser's view there are reasonable grounds for the publication 
of a dumping duty notice or a countewailing duty notice in respect of the goods 
which are the subject of the proposed application. 
5.2 
For the avoidance of doubt, clause 5.1 does not require OneSteel to take any action to 
prevent an application being under Part XVB, Division 2 of the Customs Act 1901 by other 
unrelated corporations or by steel industry representatives. 
5.3 
OneSteel will pay the fees of the Adviser incurred in complying with clause 5.1.
