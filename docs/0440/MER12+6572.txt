1 
Person giving the undertaking 
1.1 
This Undertaking is given to the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) 
by SembSITA Australia Pty Ltd (ACN 002 902 660) of 3 Rider Boulevard, Rhodes, New 
South Wales on behalf of itself and its subsidiaries (together referred to as SITA) under 
section 87B of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) (Act). 
2 
Background 
The parties  
2.1 
SITA is a supplier of waste management services in Australia.  SITA provides waste 
collection services to both commercial and industrial (C&I) customers and municipal councils 
around Australia.  SITA's post collections business includes the ownership and operation of 
transfer stations, engineered landfills and alternative waste technology (AWT) facilities across 
Australia. 
2.2 
SITA owns and operates the Bedminster AWT facility in Cairns, which can accept and 
process putrescible waste from local councils as well as from a number of waste services firms 
supplying C&I collections services in the Cairns region of far northern Queensland. 
2.3 
SITA also has a long-term contractual arrangement to dispose of residual waste from the 
Bedminster AWT facility at the Mareeba landfill (which is owned and operated by the 
Tablelands Regional Council), as well as responsibility for the design and construction of the 
cells at the Mareeba landfill.  
2.4 
Thiess Services Pty Ltd (Thiess), a subsidiary of Leighton Holdings Limited, is a supplier of 
integrated water, energy, environmental, infrastructure and waste management services.  
Thiess proposes to sell its waste management services business. 
The Proposed Acquisition 
2.5 
Thiess holds a 50% interest in the Springmount joint venture, an unincorporated joint venture 
with FGF Developments No. 1 Pty Ltd (FGF Developments) for the design, construction, 
ownership and operation of the Springmount Landfill near Mareeba in far northern 
Queensland (Springmount JV).  The Springmount JV also operates the Woree Transfer 
Station, which is owned by D&M Moule Holdings Pty Ltd and leased to the Springmount JV.  
The Springmount Landfill accepts putrescible and inert waste from local councils as well as 
from waste services firms providing C&I collections.  The Woree Transfer Station accepts 
both putrescible and inert waste from waste services firms supplying C&I collection services 
in the Cairns region. 
2.6 
On 3 April 2012 SITA lodged a confidential submission with the ACCC seeking informal 
merger clearance for the acquisition (either directly or through a related body corporate) of 
Thiess' waste management business (Proposed Acquisition). 
The ACCC’s review and competition concerns 
2.7 
The ACCC considered that, in the absence of this Undertaking, the Proposed Acquisition 
would result, or would be likely to result, in a substantial lessening of competition in relation 
to the supply of putrescible waste processing and disposal services in the Cairns region.

2.8 
The ACCC considered that, post-acquisition and in the absence of this Undertaking, customers 
seeking putrescible waste processing and disposal services in the Cairns region would not 
have effective alternatives to the facilities that SITA would own, operate or otherwise have a 
significant interest in. 
The Undertaking remedy 
2.9 
SITA has, without admission, offered this Undertaking under section 87B of the Act to divest 
its interest in the Springmount JV which it will acquire as a result of the Proposed Acquisition, 
to a purchaser approved by the ACCC.  
2.10 
The objective of this Undertaking is to address the ACCC’s competition concerns that would 
otherwise arise as a result of the Proposed Acquisition. This Undertaking aims to achieve this 
objective by:  
(a) 
the maintenance of a viable, effective, stand-alone independent and long term 
competitor for the supply of putrescible waste processing and disposal services in the 
Cairns region; 
(b) 
ensuring the Approved Purchaser has the necessary assets, rights and agreements to 
compete effectively with SITA;  
(c) 
maintaining the economic viability, marketability, competitiveness and goodwill of 
the Divestiture Assets prior to divestiture;  
(d) 
requiring SITA to hold its interest in the Springmount JV separate from SITA’s other 
assets and businesses pending divestiture of the Divestiture Assets;  
(e) 
requiring SITA to appoint an Approved Independent Representative to the 
Springmount JV Committee from the Completion Date to the Divestiture Date; and 
(f) 
providing for the effective oversight of SITA’s compliance with this Undertaking.

Divestiture

providing all information necessary for the Consents to be given; 
(c) 
act in good faith in its negotiations with the Approved Purchaser in relation to 
obtaining  any Consents; 
(d)

providing the Consents; and

to any goods or services that are required for the operation of the Divestiture Assets 
which may be affected by any failure to gain a Consent.

Purchaser fails to obtain or is unable to obtain one or more Consents, then:  
(a) 
SITA must immediately provide to the ACCC, in writing details of the:

Ring Fenced Information 
7.9 
Any information requested by SITA from the Approved Independent Representative relating 
to the Divestiture Assets or the Springmount JV which it requires in order to comply with any 
obligation under this Undertaking, and which obligation is notified to the Approved 
Independent Representative, who may decide whether or not to provide access to that 
information, will be ring fenced information (Ring Fenced Information).  
7.10 
Subject to this Undertaking, SITA must, from the Completion Date, do everything within its 
power to prevent any Ring Fenced Information being communicated, disclosed to, used or 
accessed by any officer or employee of SITA other than the Approved Manager and an officer 
or employee who needs that information in order for SITA to comply with its obligations 
under this Undertaking.   
7.11 
SITA must procure that each person to whom Ring Fenced Information is communicated or 
disclosed, or by whom Ring Fenced Information is accessed or used, in accordance with this 
clause 7.11 observes the restrictions on the communication, disclosure, use and access of Ring 
Fenced Information in this Undertaking as if those restrictions were obligations of that person.  
In this regard, SITA must use best endeavours to: 
(a) 
prevent any breach of the obligation of confidentiality in relation to Ring Fenced 
Information by such a person; 
(b) 
enforce the obligation of confidentiality in relation to the Ring Fenced Information by 
such a person; and 
(c) 
prevent any further breaches of confidentiality in relation to the Ring Fenced 
Information by such a person. 
7.12 
SITA must maintain Ring Fenced Information on files and on systems that: 
(a) 
are separate from those used by personnel of SITA who are not entitled to use the 
Ring Fenced Information; and 
(b) 
prohibit access to Ring Fenced Information by personnel of SITA who are not entitled 
to use the Ring Fenced Information. 
7.13 
SITA must advise the ACCC of any breach of the obligations of confidentiality in relation to 
Ring Fenced Information as soon as SITA becomes aware of the breach and, in any event, 
within two Business Days of SITA becoming aware of the breach.
