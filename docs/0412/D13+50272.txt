The Parties to the Proposed Acquisition

section 87B of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth)from
Nestle in connection with Nestle's Proposed Acquisition of the global
infant nutrition business of Pfizer Nutrition.

Nestle has applied to the ACCC to vary the Undertaking to
require Nestle to take all steps to ensure that any licence
agreement entered into with the Approved Purchaser pursuant

result of a breach of that licence agreement by Nestle and to
include a procedure for approval by the ACCC of termination of
any such licence agreement by Nestle;
