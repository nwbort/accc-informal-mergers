BACKGROUND 
 
1 
Barloworld is a wholly owned subsidiary of Barloworld Limited and operates an 
architectural and decorative paints manufacturing and retailing business in 
Australia using brands including Taubmans, Bristol and White Knight brands 
(Barloworld A&D Business). 
2 
The Barloworld A&D Business has the third largest percentage share of the 
A&D Market. 
3 
Wattyl operates, amongst other things, an architectural and decorative paints 
and industrial paints manufacturing and retailing business in Australia, using  
brands including Solagard, Estapol, Wattyl i.d, Pascol, Solver, Granosite, 
Aspects and Killrust (Wattyl A&D and Industrial Business). 
4 
The Wattyl A&D Business has the second largest percentage share of the A&D 
Market. 
5 
On 29 March 2006, Barloworld International S.L.U (acting as the general partner 
of Barloworld Coatings LP (LP 00000480)) made an offer to shareholders in 
Wattyl to acquire from them all of the fully paid ordinary shares in Wattyl Limited 
(Takeover Offer). 
6 
If the Takeover Offer is successful and Barloworld International S.L.U obtains 
control of Wattyl it is the intention of Barloworld International S.L.U to operate 
both the Barloworld A&D Business and the Wattyl A&D Business in the A&D 
Market (Merger). 
7 
The Merger will result in Barloworld having the largest percentage share of the 
A&D Market and will result in the A&D market being dominated by two firms, 
Barloworld and Orica, which company markets, amongst others, the Dulux 
brand of paints. 
8 
In its Statement of Issues dated 23 March 2006 (Statement of Issues), the 
Commission expressed a preliminary view that the Merger is likely to 


contravene section 50 of the Trade Practices Act 1974 in that the Merger would 
be likely to lead to reduced competition and increased prices because: 
(a) 
the dominant A&D Market participants (Wattyl, Barloworld and Orica) 
are both manufacturers and retailers of A&D Paint and such integration 
by firms with such large market shares acts as a barrier to entry and 
expansion by competitors in the A&D Market; 
(b) 
consumer awareness of the dominant A&D Market participants’ A&D 
paint brands is such that new entry to the supply of A&D paints in 
Australia on a scale that would constrain the Merged Business to act 
competitively appears unlikely to occur within a two year period; 
(c) 
manufacturers of industrial and heavy duty coatings are unlikely to 
represent a credible competitive threat to the Merged Business in 
relation to the supply of A&D paints; 
(d) 
entry and competition at the margins by niche suppliers of A&D Paint is 
unlikely to provide an effective competitive constraint on the remaining 
dominant A&D Market participants; 
(e) 
trade customers’ must be in a position to supply consumers the most 
recognised brands of paint; and 
(f) 
retailers of A&D Paint and the remaining dominant A&D Market 
participants would have common incentives that would increase 
barriers to entry and expansion. 
9 
The Barloworld Parties do not accept that the Merger will, or is likely to, result in 
a substantial lessening of competition in any market.  
10 
Without admission, the Barloworld Parties have offered this Undertaking to the 
Commission with the intention that the Undertaking will address the 
Commission’s competition concerns.  Specifically, the Barloworld Parties are of 
the view that the Bristol Sale, the Distribution Agreement and the Supply 
Agreement will provide a new entrant or an existing participant in the A&D 
Market with the opportunity to overcome the barriers to entry and expansion 
identified by the Commission in its Statement of Issues by offering for sale: 
(a) 
both A&D Paint manufacturing and retail assets; and 
(b) 
the Bristol brand which is a strong and well recognised brand in 
Australia with a history in the A&D Market established over 65 years. 
11 
The Undertaking is intended to facilitate the establishment of a participant in the 
A&D Market that would effectively constrain the Merged Business to act 
competitively on a national basis across the range of A&D Paint. 
12 
The Barloworld Parties have proffered this Undertaking to the Commission 
pursuant to section 87B of the Act.

UNDERTAKINGS 
 
Acquisition of Wattyl 
53 
The Barloworld parties will not proceed with the Merger until this Undertaking is 
accepted by the Commission. 
Divestment of the Bristol Assets 
54 
The Barloworld Parties will not sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of the Bristol 
Assets otherwise than to an Approved Purchaser in accordance with this 
Undertaking and will not, without the prior written consent of the Commission, 
remove or make any material adverse change to such assets. 


55 
The Barloworld Parties must divest the Bristol Assets and must use all 
reasonable endeavours to do so in accordance with this Undertaking within 6 
months of the Merger Date and otherwise in accordance with this Undertaking. 
Implementation of the Bristol Sale 
56 
In accordance with its obligations to divest the Bristol Assets the Barloworld 
Parties will do all things within their power or control to enter into an agreement 
or agreements with the Approved Purchaser within 6 months of the Merger Date 
pursuant to which the Barloworld Parties or any one of them will agree to 
implement the Bristol Sale upon reasonable commercial terms, such terms to be 
subject to Commission Approval. 
Toll Manufacturing Agreement 
57 
In the event that the Approved Purchaser elects as part of the Bristol Sale not to 
purchase the Manufacturing Facility, and if required by the Approved Purchaser, 
the Barloworld Parties will do all things within their power or control to enter into 
an agreement with the Approved Purchaser on reasonable commercial terms 
(such terms to be subject to Commission Approval) pursuant to which 
Barloworld will agree to manufacture such A&D Paint as the Approved 
Purchaser shall require for a period of up to 5 years.   
Distribution Agreement 
58 
The Barloworld Parties will, on reasonable commercial terms, offer to, and if 
required to will, receive on consignment and make available for sale the 
Approved Purchaser’s A&D Paint products in retail outlets owned or controlled 
by the Merged Business for a period of 2 years.   
Supply Agreement 
59 
If required by the Approved Purchaser, the Barloworld Parties will do all things 
in their power or control to enter into a Supply Agreement with the Approved 
Purchaser, upon reasonable commercial terms in the A&D Market (such terms 
to be subject to Commission Approval) for a period of 2 years.  
60 
Pursuant to the Supply Agreement, the Barloworld Parties will supply on a 
consignment basis to the Approved Purchaser those Stock Keeping Units 
(SKUs) manufactured by or on behalf of the Barloworld Parties that were sold in 
stores branded with the Bristol Trade Marks as at the Merger Date (other than 
Bristol A&D branded paint), which are listed in Schedule 6. 
Bristol Employees 
61 
To facilitate the acceptance by the Commission of the Bristol Employees the 
Barloworld Parties will provide the Commission with:  
(a) 
a list of all employees of Barloworld who, as at the date of this 
Undertaking, are employed exclusively in the manufacture, 


warehousing, sale and marketing of the Bristol brand, which 
employees shall be made available to the Approved Purchaser in 
accordance with the terms of this Undertaking; 
(b) 
a list of all employees who, as at the date of this Undertaking, work at 
the Bristol Stores, which employees shall be made available to the 
Approved Purchaser in accordance with the terms of this Undertaking; 
(c) 
a list all employees of Barloworld who, as at the date of this 
Undertaking, are employed on a non-exclusive basis in the sales and 
marketing of the Bristol brand; and 
(d) 
a list identifying which of the Bristol employees referred to in sub 
paragraph (c) above have been identified by the Barloworld Parties as 
being necessary for the on going conduct of the Bristol Business and 
which employees have not been so identified and the reasons for such 
identification and non-identification as the case may be.  
62 
The Barloworld Parties agree that they will not enforce or rely upon any 
provision of the employment arrangement of a Bristol Employee, which requires 
that the employee not accept employment with a competitor of the Barloworld 
Parties, should that particular employee seek or be offered employment with the 
Approved Purchaser in respect of the Bristol Business. 
63 
The Barloworld Parties agree not to take any steps to attempt to prevent or 
dissuade Bristol Employees from seeking, accepting or continuing employment 
with the Approved Purchaser in respect of the Bristol Business.  
64 
Nothing in this Undertaking shall be construed as requiring any Bristol 
Employee to accept employment with the Approved Purchaser. 
65 
Subject to paragraph 63 of this Undertaking, nothing in this Undertaking shall be 
construed as preventing or limiting the right of the Barloworld Parties or any one 
of them to offer to or to re-employ any Bristol Employee should such employee’s 
employment with the Approved Purchaser be subsequently terminated for 
whatever reason. 
Maintenance of the Bristol Business 
66 
Until completion of the Bristol Sale in accordance with this Undertaking, the 
Barloworld Parties will take all reasonable steps within their power and control 
as are necessary to: 
(a) 
preserve the Bristol Business as an on-going viable business in the 
A&D Market; 
(b) 
maintain the standards of manufacture, distribution, promotion and 
sale of the Bristol products which are current as at the date of this 
Undertaking; 


(c) 
continue to manufacture using the Manufacturing Facility, distribute, 
promote and sell the range of Bristol products which are manufactured, 
distributed, promoted and sold as at the date of this Undertaking and, 
as far as is reasonably possible, in quantities no less than the 
quantities which are manufactured, distributed, promoted and sold as 
at the date of this Undertaking; 
(d) 
ensure that the Bristol Employees and any other employees involved in 
the manufacture, distribution, promotion and sale of the Bristol 
products continue to be employed in the conduct the Bristol Business; 
(e) 
ensure that the Manufacturing Facility is preserved in the same 
working order as it is at the date of this Undertaking;  
(f) 
ensure that the Bristol Assets are preserved;  
(g) 
maintain all current contracts and agreements in respect of the Bristol 
Business; 
(h) 
maintain all levels of promotional, advertising, sales and technical 
assistance, marketing and merchandising support for the Bristol 
Business as are current at the date of this Undertaking; 
(i) 
acquire and pay for sufficient and timely deliveries of all goods and 
services required by the Bristol Business;  
(j) 
maintain all books and records relating to the Bristol Business; and 
(k) 
maintain all information necessary to enable the divestiture of the 
Bristol Assets, 
in accordance with past practice of the Barloworld Parties. 
 
COMMISSION APPROVAL OF BRISTOL SALE 
 
67 
To facilitate the Commission’s approval of the Approved Purchaser the 
Barloworld Parties must provide the Commission (if it has not already done so), 
on a confidential basis, with: 
(a) 
an Information Brochure relating to the Bristol Sale which must contain 
a detailed description of Bristol Business, the proposed transaction and 
all land, premises, plant, equipment, inventory and other assets that 
comprise the Bristol Sale, together with confirmation that a copy of this 
Undertaking has been provided to the Proposed Purchasers together 
with details of the postal address for the general manager, Mergers 
and Asset Sales Branch of the Commission; 
(b)  
a copy of a draft agreement or agreements for effecting the Bristol Sale 
in accordance with this Undertaking;  


(c) 
a list of all prospective purchasers who have expressed a serious 
interest in the Bristol Sale (Proposed Purchasers); and 
(d) 
a memorandum of understanding, if one is proposed to be entered into 
by a prospective purchaser and the Barloworld Parties. 
68 
The Commission will, within 7 days of the date of receipt of the documents 
referred to in paragraph 67 above, notify the Barloworld Parties in writing that it 
either: 
(a) 
approves one or more Proposed Purchasers and the terms of the draft 
agreement or agreements for effecting the Bristol Sale in accordance 
with this Undertaking ; or 
(b) 
does not approve any of the Proposed Purchasers, 
and where it does not approve a Proposed Purchaser and/or the terms of any 
draft agreement or agreements for effecting the Bristol Sale in accordance with 
this Undertaking, it must give reasons for such non approval. 
69 
In the event that the Commission does not approve a particular Proposed 
Purchaser, the Barloworld Parties shall be free to submit a further list containing 
the names of other Proposed Purchasers, in which case the provisions of 
paragraphs 67 and 68 of this Undertaking relating to Commission approval shall 
apply. 
70 
In the event that the Commission does not approve a particular draft agreement 
or agreements for effecting the Bristol Sale the Barloworld Parties shall be free 
to submit a further draft agreement or agreements for effecting the Bristol Sale, 
in which case the provisions of paragraphs 67 and 68 of this Undertaking 
relating to Commission approval shall apply. 
71 
In the event that no Approved Purchaser exists on a date 6 months after the 
Merger Date the Barloworld Parties will identify a sales agent for the Bristol 
Business (Proposed Agent). 
72 
The Proposed Agent must be a person who is independent of the Barloworld 
Parties. 
73 
The Barloworld Parties will advise the Commission in writing, within 5 business 
days of the Barloworld Parties identifying the Proposed Agent of the identity of 
the Proposed Agent together with such information that the Barloworld Parties 
consider the Commission reasonably requires to assess whether the 
Commission is prepared to consent to the Proposed Agent. 
74 
If, within 5 business days of receipt by the Commission of the information from 
the Barloworld Parties referred to in paragraph 73 above: 
(a) 
the Commission does not object to the Proposed Agent, the Barloworld 
Parties will appoint the proposed Agent as soon as practicable 


thereafter (Approved Agent) and forward to the Commission a copy of 
the executed terms of appointment; or 
(b) 
the Commission does object to the Proposed Agent, the Barloworld 
Parties must either (at the Commission’s election): 
(i) 
identify within 10 days an alternative Proposed Agent for the 
Bristol Sale (whose appointment will be considered by the 
Commission in accordance with paragraphs 73 and74); or 
(ii) 
appoint a nominee of the president of the Institute of 
Chartered Accountants of Australia as the Proposed Agent 
and the Barloworld Parties must appoint him or her to carry 
out the functions of the Approved Agent. 
75 
Within 5 business days of the appointment of the Approved Agent, the 
Barloworld Parties must: 
(a) 
procure and/or grant the Approved Agent, by execution and provision 
of an unconditional and irrevocable power of attorney in favour of the 
Approved Agent, all necessary power and authority to divest, sell and 
transfer the Bristol Assets and to implement the Bristol Sale and, 
subject to subparagraph (c) below, to do so in the manner and on the 
terms considered appropriate by the Approved Agent, in its sole 
discretion, to ensure compliance with this Undertaking;  
(b) 
require that the Approved Agent use his or her best endeavours to 
implement the Bristol Sale: 
(i) 
within 4 months from the date of the power of attorney 
granted by the Barloworld Parties pursuant to this 
Undertaking; and 
(ii) 
at the most favourable price and terms reasonably available 
to the Approved Agent at the time, but subject to an absolute 
and unconditional obligation to implement the Bristol Sale at 
no minimum price;  and 
(c) 
in the case of any Distribution Agreement and Supply Agreement to be 
entered into with a purchaser who is identified by the Approved Agent 
pursuant to this paragraph, the terms of such agreements shall be 
subject to Commission Approval.  
76 
The Barloworld Parties will provide the Approved Agent with: 
(a) 
all relevant information available to the Barloworld Parties; and 
(b) 
all reasonable assistance, 
to enable the Approved Agent to divest, sell and transfer the Bristol Assets and 
implement the Bristol Sale. 


77 
The Barloworld Parties will indemnify (save for fraud, negligence, breach of 
contract or breach of duty) the Approved Agent in the course of carrying out his 
or her functions in accordance with the terms of his or her appointment.   
78 
The Commission agrees that a sale of the Bristol Assets in accordance with the 
provisions of paragraphs 67 to 77 inclusive of this Undertaking shall constitute 
compliance by the Barloworld Parties with the terms of this Undertaking. 
 
OBLIGATION TO PROVIDE THE COMMISSION WITH INFORMATION 
 
79 
The Barloworld Parties shall provide the Commission with copies of any 
agreements with the Approved Purchaser and the Approved Agent referred to in 
this Undertaking, such documents to be treated as confidential by the 
Commission. 
 
AUDIT 
 
80 
The Barloworld Parties will engage at their expense an auditor (Auditor) to 
provide to the Commission every 90 days from the date of this Undertaking until 
the Bristol Sale is implemented, a report on the Barloworld parties’ compliance 
with their obligations under this Undertaking and any qualifications made by the 
Auditor in forming its view and any recommendations made by the Auditor to 
improve the integrity of the audit process. 
81 
The Barloworld Parties will promptly identify the proposed Auditor and provide 
the Commission with information in relation to the expertise of the Auditor and 
information in relation to the Auditor’s independence of the Barloworld Parties.  
The Auditor must be approved by the Commission, which approval will not be 
unreasonably withheld. 
82 
The Barloworld Parties undertake to use their best endeavours to implement 
any recommendations of the Auditor as soon as reasonably practicable after the 
release of the relevant Auditor’s report. 
83 
The Barloworld Parties will cooperate fully with the Auditor and will provide the 
Auditor with such information and assistance as is necessary to enable the 
Auditor to carry out his or her functions as set out in this Undertaking. 
84 
The Barloworld Parties will authorise the Auditor to engage any technical 
expertise as the Auditor reasonably requires to conduct the audits.

Undertaking available for public inspection and that the Commission will from 
time to time publish and publicly refer to this Undertaking.
