Recitals
A.
On 11 March 2006, the Commission accepted undertakings from Toll Holdings Limited
("Toll") in accordance with section 87B of the Trade Practices Act 1974 (the "Act") to address
competition concerns raised by the Commission as a result of Toll's acquisition of Patrick
Corporation Limited (the "Original Undertakings").
B.
The Original Undertakings were varied with the Commission's consent on 4 May 2006, 11
September 2006, 9 October 2006, 13 December 2006 and 18 April 2007. (The undertakings as
varied are referred to as the "Undertakings").
C.
The variation to the Undertakings consented to by the Commission on 18 April 2007 (the
"Fifth Variation"), provided for (among other things) a waiver of the obligation under the
Original Undertakings that Toll divest 50% of Pacific National (the "PN Interest"). This
waiver is predicated on implementation of a restructure of the Toll group and transfer of
Pacific National to a new separate and independent entity, namely Asciano Limited
("Restructure").
D.
The Restructure will not occur until a series of steps provided for in the Undertakings are
satisfied including resolution by Toll shareholders to implement a scheme of arrangement to
give effect to the Restructure. Until such time as the Restructure is implemented, the
obligations under the Original Undertakings to hold separate from Toll and otherwise preserve
the PN Interest remains in effect and Toll is required to comply with these obligations.
E.
On 31 October 2006, the State of Victoria and Pacific National entered into a non-binding
terms sheet providing for the reacquisition by the State of Victoria of the Victorian regional
rail network from Pacific National. On 13 April 2007, Pacific National entered into an
agreement with relevant agencies of the Victorian Government to sell the Victorian regional
rail network to the State of Victoria.
F.
This variation (the "Sixth Variation") amends the Undertakings so as to enable the sale of the
Victorian rail network without Toll contravening the hold separate and business preservation
obligations in respect of the PN Interest under the Undertakings.
G.
The Commission has agreed, pursuant to Section 87B of the Act, to accept this Variation.
1. 
Commencement
This Sixth Variation comes into effect when:
(a)
this Sixth Variation is executed by Toll; and
(b)
the Commission accepts this Sixth Variation so executed.
2. 
Insert new clause 7A - Victorian Network Assets
A new clause 7A in the following terms is to be inserted following clause 7:
"7A. 
Victorian Network Assets
Nothing in this Undertaking precludes the transfer, by sale or otherwise, of the
Victorian Network Assets to the State of Victoria to the extent such transfer
proceeds in accordance with the terms set out in the Asset Surrender and Sale
Agreement, and any such transfer will not constitute a contravention of any
obligation in these Undertakings."

3. 
Variation to clause 19 - Definitions and Interpretation
Clause 19.1 is varied by the insertion of the following definitions to be included in the list of
definitions in clause 19.1 in alphabetical order:
"Asset Surrender and Sale Agreement means the Asset Surrender and Sale Agreement
between Pacific National (Victoria) Ltd., Pacific National (ACT) Ltd., Victorian Rail Track,
The Director of Public Transport, Pacific National and V/Line Passenger Pty. Ltd dated 13
April 2007 and documentation referred to therein."
"Victorian Network Assets means the assets and liabilities of the Victorian regional rail
network proposed to be surrendered or transferred by Pacific National to the State of Victoria
as set out in the Asset Surrender and Sale Agreement."
