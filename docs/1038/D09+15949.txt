Background
A 
On 27 May 2008, the Commission accepted the Original Undertaking offered by
Video Ezy pursuant to section 87B of the Act in order to resolve issues raised by the
Commission in relation to free-to-air and pay television commercials for the Relevant
Titles, and the SMS advertisements for 'Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix'
and 'The Simpsons Movie', in a non-contentious and timely manner.
B 
Upon review of Video Ezy's Compliance Program, the Commission raised concerns
that Video Ezy was not intending to engage the Compliance Instructor to deliver
training at the induction sessions of relevant staff.
C 
As Video Ezy considers it would be impractical and unnecessarily costly to engage
the Compliance Instructor to deliver training at induction sessions, Video Ezy has
sought this Variation so that Video Ezy is not required to engage the Compliance
Instructor to deliver trade practices training at induction sessions.
D 
The Commission is satisfied that this Variation is appropriate.
1 
Definitions
1.1 
In this Variation, unless the contrary intention appears, any term defined in the
Original Undertaking and not defined in this Variation has the meaning given in the
Original Undertaking.
1.2 
In this Variation, the following definitions apply:
Original Undertaking means all of the provisions of the undertaking, including its
annexures, accepted by the Commission pursuant to section 87B of the Act on
27 May 2008.
Variation means all of the provisions of this document.
2 
Commencement of Variation
2.1 
This Variation comes into effect when:
2.1.1 
the Variation is executed by Video Ezy; and
2.1.2 
the Variation so executed is accepted by the Commission.
3 
Variation to clause 1.1 - Definitions
3.1 
Clause 1.1 of the Original Undertaking is amended as follows:
3.1.1 
by varying the definition of "Undertaking" by inserting the words ", as
varied from time to time in accordance with section 87B(2) of the Act" after
the words "including its Attachments and Annexures"; and
4 
Variation to clause 5 of Annexure A - Compliance Training
4.1 
Clause 5 of Annexure A to the Original Undertaking is amended by deleting the words
"and at Staff inductions".
