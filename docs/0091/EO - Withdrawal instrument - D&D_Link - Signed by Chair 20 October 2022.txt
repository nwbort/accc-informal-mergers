Withdrawal of Undertaking to the Australian Competition 
and Consumer Commission 
Consent to withdraw undertaking given under section 87B of the Competition and 
Consumer Act 2010 by Dye & Durham Limited (Ontario Corp No. 002762926) and 
its subsidiary Dye & Durham Holdings Pty Ltd ACN 646 897 412 
Background 
(1) 
On 8 September 2022, the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission 
(ACCC) announced it had accepted an undertaking given by Dye & Durham 
Limited (Ontario Corp No. 002762926) and its subsidiary Dye & Durham 
Holdings Pty Ltd ACN 646 897 412 (D&D) pursuant to section 87B of the 
Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) (the Act) (the Undertaking) in 
connection with D&D’s proposal to acquire 100% of the share capital in Link 
Administration Holdings Limited (the Proposed Acquisition). 
(2) 
On 19 October 2022, D&D sought the ACCC’s consent to the withdrawal of the 
Undertaking pursuant to s 87B(2) of the Act. 
(3)  Consent is sought on the basis that the Proposed Acquisition is no longer 
proceeding.   
Withdrawal of the Undertaking 
(4)  In accordance with section 87B(2) of the Act, the Undertaking is withdrawn in its 
entirety with effect on and from the date on which the ACCC gives its consent to 
the withdrawal.
