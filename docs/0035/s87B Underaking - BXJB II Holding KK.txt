Person giving the Undertaking 
1.1 
This Undertaking is given to the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) 
by BXJB II Holding KK (0104-01-182081) (referred to as BXJB in this Undertaking) on behalf 
of itself and its subsidiaries.

Background 
Parties to the proposed acquisition 
BXJB  
2.1 
Blackstone Capital Partners VIII L.P. and Blackstone Capital Partners Asia II L.P. indirectly 
own Nucleus Network Pty Limited (Nucleus). Nucleus supplies phase I clinical trial services 
to biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies, based both in Australia and internationally. 
2.2 
Blackstone Capital Partners Asia II L.P indirectly owns BXJB. 
I'rom  
2.3 
I'rom Group Co., Ltd. (I'rom) is a healthcare business listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange 
that specialises in the research and development of medicine and gene technology. I’rom 
provides services to medical institutions conducting clinical trials, including site management 
and CRO services. 
2.4 
In Australia, I’rom owns (indirectly) CMAX Clinical Research Pty Ltd (CMAX) which supplies 
phase 1 clinical trial services to biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies, based both in 
Australia and internationally.  
Proposed Acquisition 
2.5 
BXJB proposes to acquire 100% of the shares of I'rom, with Mr Toyotaka Mori (Founder, 
President and current CEO of I’rom) subsequently acquiring from BXJB 45% of the shares in 
I’rom by way of share purchase, capital contribution or in another equivalent manner (the 
Proposed Acquisition). 
2.6 
BXJB intends to release its tender offer in connection with the Proposed Acquisition shortly 
after the Commencement Date. The tender offer period will expire no less than 20 Business 
Days after release of the offer. 
2.7 
Following the Share Acquisition Date, BXJB will: 
(a) "squeeze out" any remaining minority shareholders who do not tender their shares, 
pursuant to relevant provisions under the Japan Companies Act; and 
(b) then appoint new directors to the Board of I'rom. 
2.8 
The Control Date is anticipated to occur approximately four months from the commencement 
of the tender offer.  
The ACCC’s review  
2.9 
On 24 July 2024, the ACCC commenced its public review of the Proposed Acquisition.  
2.10 
The ACCC undertook market inquiries and considered information provided by the parties to 
the Proposed Acquisition, industry participants and others. The purpose of the ACCC’s 
inquiries was to assess whether the Proposed Acquisition would have the effect, or be likely 
to have the effect, of substantially lessening competition in any market in Australia in 
contravention of section 50 of the Act. 


 
 
 
4 
 
 
The ACCC’s competition concerns  
2.11 
The ACCC was concerned that, in the absence of the Undertaking, the Proposed Acquisition 
may have the effect, or be likely to have the effect, of substantially lessening competition in 
the supply of phase 1 clinical trial services in Australia, and in the supply of CRO services in 
Australia. 
2.12 
The ACCC was concerned that in the absence of the Undertaking, the Proposed Acquisition 
would bring the two largest providers of phase 1 clinical trial services in Australia under 
common ownership and was likely to significantly lessen the competitive constraints on these 
providers. The ACCC was concerned that this would have likely led to the prices of phase 1 
clinical trial services in Australia being higher and/or the quality of those services being lower, 
than they would absent the Proposed Acquisition. 
2.13 
The ACCC was also concerned that, in the absence of the Undertaking, Blackstone may have 
the ability and incentive to foreclose CROs that compete with the Precision for Medicine 
Group (which is majority owned by Blackstone) by restricting their access to phase 1 clinical 
trial services provided by Nucleus and CMAX. This may have the effect of substantially 
lessening competition in the provision of services by CROs. 
The Undertaking remedy 
2.14 
BXJB does not consider that the Proposed Acquisition would have the effect, or be likely to 
have the effect, of substantially lessening competition in any relevant market. However, in 
order to address the ACCC’s competition concerns, BXJB has offered this Undertaking 
pursuant to section 87B of the Act to divest all of the shares held indirectly by I'rom in CMAX.  
2.15 
The objective of this Undertaking is to address the ACCC's competition concerns as set out 
above that would otherwise arise as a consequence of the Proposed Acquisition. The 
Undertaking aims to achieve this objective by placing obligations on BXJB: 
(a) to ensure that the Divestiture Business is sold to an Approved Purchaser that will result in 
the maintenance of a viable, effective, stand-alone, independent and long-term competitor 
for phase 1 clinical trial services in Australia; 
(b) to ensure the purchaser of the Divestiture Business has all the necessary associated 
assets and rights to compete effectively with Nucleus in the supply of phase 1 clinical trial 
services in Australia; 
(c) to maintain the independence and economic viability, marketability, competitiveness and 
goodwill of the Divestiture Business prior to divestiture; and  
(d) to provide for the effective oversight of BXJB's compliance with this Undertaking.

Divestiture of the Divestiture Business 
Divestiture  
5.1 
BXJB must, in accordance with this Undertaking, divest, or cause the divestiture of, the 
Divestiture Business only to an Approved Purchaser.   
5.2 
In the event that clause 10 applies, BXJB must not authorise the Approved Divestiture Agent 
to divest the Unsold Business to a purchaser other than an Approved Purchaser. 
5.3 
BXJB must divest, or cause the divestiture of, the Divestiture Business by: 
(a) the sale of all of the shares in the Divestiture Business to the Approved Purchaser, 
pursuant to the Approved Sale and Purchase Agreement and within the Initial Sale 
Period, otherwise clause 10 applies; 
(b) the transfer or grant to the Approved Purchaser of all Consents pursuant to clauses 5.5 to 
5.6;  
(c) the transfer of any Transferred Personnel required pursuant to clauses 5.8 to 5.9; 
(d) compliance with any Approved Transitional Technical Assistance Agreement required 
pursuant to clauses 5.10 to 5.12; and 
(e) compliance with any Approved Transitional Supply Agreement required pursuant to 
clauses 5.13 to 5.15. 
5.4 
From the Share Acquisition Date, BXJB must take all reasonable steps available to it to effect, 
as soon as possible: 
(a)  the occurrence of the Control Date; and 
(b) completion of the divestiture of the Divestiture Business to an Approved Purchaser.

Consents  
5.5 
BXJB must:  
(a) obtain or assist the Approved Purchaser to obtain as expeditiously as possible, all 
Consents as required before completion of the divestiture of the Divestiture Business; 
(b) comply with all requirements necessary to obtain any Consents, including by promptly 
providing all information necessary for the Consents to be given;  
(c) act in good faith in its negotiations with the Approved Purchaser in relation to obtaining 
any Consents;  
(d) promptly pay the costs and expenses of any third party reasonably incurred in providing 
the Consents, with the exception of any filing fee payable by the Approved Purchaser to 
the Foreign Investment Review Board (FIRB) or any other regulator subject to a 
mandatory notification obligation (as applicable); and  
(e) enter an agreement with the Approved Purchaser and approved by the ACCC or vary any 
existing Approved Transitional Supply Agreement as required by the Approved 
Purchaser, in relation to any goods or services that are required for the operation of the 
Divestiture Business which may be affected by any failure to gain a Consent. 
5.6 
If, seven Business Days before completion of the divestiture of the Divestiture Business, the 
Approved Purchaser does not or is otherwise unable to obtain one or more Consents, then 
BXJB must:  
(a) immediately provide to the ACCC in writing details of the:  
(i) 
Consents that have not been obtained;  
(ii) 
reasons why the Consents have not been obtained; and  
(iii) 
information or material required to obtain the Consents. 
(b) continue to do everything in its power to satisfy clause 5.5 as soon as possible after the 
completion of the divestiture of the Divestiture Business (and until such time as clause 5.5 
is satisfied). 
5.7 
Even if BXJB has complied with clauses 5.5 to 5.6, BXJB will be in breach of this Undertaking 
if BXJB is unable to effect the divestiture of the Divestiture Business by reason that one or 
more Consents are not obtained. 
Transferred Personnel  
5.8 
At the option of the Approved Purchaser, BXJB must transfer to the Approved Purchaser:  
(a) all employees; and 
(b) all service providers under a contract for service; 
of the Divestiture Business who are, in the view of the Approved Purchaser, required for the 
Approved Purchaser to maintain, operate or conduct effectively the Divestiture Business and 
who consent to the transfer of their employment or contract for service to the Approved 
Purchaser (Transferred Personnel). 
5.9 
When fulfilling its obligations under clause 5.8, BXJB must:  
(a) encourage all Transferred Personnel to remain with the Divestiture Business, including 
offering incentives based on industry practice;  


 
 
 
7 
 
 
(b) not directly or indirectly discourage any Transferred Personnel from any obligations to 
provide services to the Divestiture Business; 
(c) effective on the date of the divestiture of the Divestiture Business, release the Transferred 
Personnel from any obligations to provide services to BXJB;  
(d) effective on the date of the divestiture of the Divestiture Business, release the Transferred 
Personnel from any non-compete or similar restraint of trade obligation, to the extent that 
such an obligation would otherwise prevent the person from performing his or her 
contemplated role in relation to the Divestiture Business or Unsold Business; and 
(e) not procure, promote or encourage the transfer of any of the Transferred Personnel from 
the Approved Purchaser to BXJB for a period of 12 months after the completion of the 
divestiture of the Divestiture Business. 
Technical Assistance 
5.10 
At the option of the Approved Purchaser, BXJB must supply to the Approved Purchaser, 
under an Approved Transitional Technical Assistance Agreement, any Technical Assistance 
that is required by the Approved Purchaser in order to operate the Divestiture Business. 
5.11 
BXJB must ensure that any Approved Transitional Technical Assistance Agreement, and any 
renewal or extension of an Approved Transitional Technical Assistance Agreement, provides 
for the supply of Technical Assistance:  
(a) on a transitional basis for a period that is nominated by the Approved Purchaser and 
approved in writing by the ACCC; and 
(b) at cost price and otherwise on arm’s length terms.  
5.12 
To avoid doubt, BXJB must seek prior written approval from the ACCC of any renewal or 
extension of an Approved Transitional Technical Assistance Agreement. Without limiting the 
ACCC’s discretion in making a decision on whether to approve a renewal or extension of an 
Approved Transitional Technical Assistance Agreement, the ACCC will have regard to the 
criteria set out in clauses 5.10 and 5.11. 
Transitional Supply Agreements  
5.13 
At the option of the Approved Purchaser, BXJB must ensure the continued supply by BXJB to 
the Approved Purchaser, under an Approved Transitional Supply Agreement, of any goods or 
services that are required by the Approved Purchaser in order for the Approved Purchaser to 
be established as a viable, effective, stand-alone, independent and long-term competitor for 
phase 1 clinical trial services in Australia. 
5.14 
BXJB must ensure that any Approved Transitional Supply Agreement and any renewal or 
extension of an Approved Transitional Supply Agreement:  
(a) is for a reasonable transitional period, to be nominated by the Approved Purchaser and 
approved in writing by the ACCC; 
(b) provides for the supply of the included goods and services at cost price; and 
(c) is on such terms other than price which are no less favourable to the Approved Purchaser 
than arm's length terms.  
5.15 
To avoid doubt, BXJB must seek prior written approval from the ACCC of any renewal or 
extension of an Approved Transitional Supply Agreement. Without limiting the ACCC’s 
discretion in making a decision on whether to approve a renewal or extension of an Approved 
Transitional Supply Agreement, the ACCC will have regard to the criteria set out in clauses 
5.13 and 5.14.

Process for approving a proposed purchaser 
Potential purchasers  
6.1 
BXJB must provide the ACCC and Approved Independent Auditor with:  
(a) the identity of any person who expresses an interest in acquiring the Divestiture Business;  
(b) the status of negotiations with each person; and  
(c) a copy of each person's offer to acquire the Divestiture Business, where relevant;  
at the following times:  
(d) as soon as possible following the Commencement Date for those persons who express 
interest before the Commencement Date;  
(e) after the Commencement Date, within 10 Business Days of each person expressing the 
interest and/or providing an offer;  
regardless of whether the person subsequently withdraws or is declined. 
Provision of a notice for a Proposed Purchaser  
6.2 
To seek ACCC approval for a Proposed Purchaser, BXJB or the Approved Divestiture Agent 
must provide the ACCC with a notice in the form prescribed in Schedule 2 to this Undertaking 
(Proposed Purchaser Notice), including a draft sale and purchase agreement, a draft 
transitional technical assistance agreement (where required by the Proposed Purchaser), and 
a draft transitional supply agreement (where required by the Proposed Purchaser). 
6.3 
The Proposed Purchaser Notice must be provided to the ACCC in accordance with clause 2.1 
of Confidential Schedule 5. 
Approval of a Proposed Purchaser after the Commencement Date 
6.4 
The ACCC may, in its discretion, approve or reject in writing the Proposed Purchaser 
identified in the Proposed Purchaser Notice. In exercising this discretion, the ACCC will 
consider, without limitation:  
(a) the draft sale and purchase agreement attached to the Proposed Purchaser Notice; 
(b) any draft transitional technical assistance agreement attached to the Proposed Purchaser 
Notice in accordance with the criteria in clauses 5.10 and 5.11; and 
(c) any draft transitional supply agreement attached to the Proposed Purchaser Notice, in 
accordance with the criteria in clauses 5.13 and 5.14. 
6.5 
Without limiting the ACCC’s discretion, in making the decision pursuant to clause 6.4, the 
factors to which the ACCC may have regard include whether the:  
(a) draft sale and purchase agreement is consistent with this Undertaking;  
(b) Proposed Purchaser will complete the transaction as contemplated by the draft sale and 
purchase agreement;  
(c) Proposed Purchaser is independent of BXJB;  
(d) Proposed Purchaser is of good financial standing;  
(e) Proposed Purchaser has a history of competition law compliance;  


 
 
 
9 
 
 
(f) Proposed Purchaser has an intention to maintain and operate the Divestiture Business as 
a going concern;  
(g) Proposed Purchaser is able to conduct the Divestiture Business effectively; and  
(h) divestiture of the Divestiture Business to the Proposed Purchaser will address any 
competition concerns of the ACCC, including in relation to the likely long-term viability and 
competitiveness of the Divestiture Business under the ownership of the Proposed 
Purchaser. 
6.6 
The ACCC may revoke an Approved Purchaser’s status as the Approved Purchaser if the 
ACCC becomes aware that any information provided to it was incorrect, inaccurate or 
misleading.

Divestiture Business Protection 
Protection of the Divestiture Business  
7.1 
From the Share Acquisition Date, BXJB must not sell or transfer its interest, or any assets 
comprising part of, or used in, the Divestiture Business (other than the sale of goods and 
services in the ordinary course of business) or make any Material Change except in 
accordance with this Undertaking or (subject to the other terms of this Undertaking) as 
necessary to allow completion of the Proposed Acquisition. 
BXJB's obligations in relation to the Divestiture Business prior to completion of divestiture  
7.2 
Without limiting this clause 7, BXJB must, from the Control Date until completion of the 
divestiture of the Divestiture Business (and, to the extent it is within its power to do so, from 
the Share Acquisition Date to the Control Date), take all steps available to it to:   
(a) ensure that the Divestiture Business is managed and operated by the CMAX executive 
management team in the ordinary course of business as a fully operational, competitive 
going concern and in such a way that preserves the economic viability, marketability, 
competitiveness and goodwill of the Divestiture Business as at the Share Acquisition 
Date; 
(b) continue to provide access to working capital and sources of credit for the Divestiture 
Business in a manner which is consistent with the financing of the Divestiture Business 
before the Share Acquisition Date; 
(c) continue to provide administrative and technical support for the Divestiture Business in a 
manner which is consistent with the operation of the Divestiture Business before the 
Share Acquisition Date and in accordance with any plans established before the Share 
Acquisition Date; 
(d) continue existing Agreements relating to the Divestiture Business with customers, 
suppliers and/or other third parties that are in place at the Share Acquisition Date; 
(e) renew or replace upon expiry Material Contracts for the provision of goods or services to 
the Divestiture Business on commercial terms favourable to the Divestiture Business; 
(f) maintain the supply of those goods and services that are part of the Divestiture Business 
to existing customers in a manner consistent with the supply of those goods and services 
as at the Share Acquisition Date; 
(g) maintain the standard of manufacture, distribution, promotion and sale of those products 
which form part of the Divestiture Business as at the Share Acquisition Date; 


 
 
 
10 
 
 
(h) promote and market the products that form part of the Divestiture Business in accordance 
with any plans established before the Share Acquisition Date; and 
(i) carry out such other actions or plans that the Approved Independent Manager considers 
to be necessary or convenient to maintain the Divestiture Business as an effectively 
competitive going concern. 
Personnel of BXJB 
7.3 
From the Control Date until completion of the divestiture of the Divestiture Business (and, to 
the extent it is within its power to do so, from the Share Acquisition Date to the Control Date), 
BXJB must: 
(a) in consultation with the Approved Independent Manager, replace any: 
(i) 
Transferred Personnel; or 
(ii) 
if the Transferred Personnel have not yet been identified, any personnel 
necessary for the operation of the Divestiture Business;

(b) not terminate or vary the terms of employment or engagement (or agree to do any of

(i) 
Transferred Personnel; or 
(ii) 
if the Transferred Personnel have not yet been identified, any personnel 
necessary for the operation of the Divestiture Business; 
except where the termination is for cause, is done in consultation with the Approved 
Independent Manager, and the Divestiture Business' standard employment policies and 
procedures are complied with; and  
(c) not directly or indirectly procure, promote or encourage the redeployment of personnel 
necessary for the operation of the Divestiture Business as at the Share Acquisition Date 
to any other business operated by BXJB.

Independent Management of the Divestiture Business 
Obligation to appoint an Approved Independent Manager 
8.1 
BXJB must appoint and maintain an Approved Independent Manager to manage the 
Divestiture Business, from the Control Date until the completion of the divestiture of the

Process for approving a Proposed Independent Manager 
8.2

8.3 
If clauses 8.12, 8.13 or 8.14 apply, BXJB must provide the ACCC with a Proposed

Manager identified in the Proposed Independent Manager Notice. 
8.5 
Without limiting the ACCC’s discretion, in deciding whether to approve a Proposed

(b) person named in the Proposed Independent Manager Notice or identified by the ACCC is 
sufficiently independent of BXJB;  
(c) draft terms of appointment and the draft separation and management plan are consistent 
with this Undertaking; and 
(d) draft terms of appointment and the draft separation and management plan are otherwise 
acceptable to the ACCC. 
Appointment of the Approved Independent Manager 
8.6 
 After receiving a written notice from the ACCC of its approval of the Proposed Independent 
Manager, the draft terms of appointment and draft separation and management plan, BXJB 
must by the Control Date: 
(a) appoint the person approved by the ACCC as the Approved Independent Manager on the 
Approved Terms of Appointment; and 
(b) forward to the ACCC a copy of the executed Approved Terms of Appointment.

(d) make only those Material Changes to the Divestiture Business which the ACCC does not

(e) operate and manage the Divestiture Business to the maximum extent practicable, in a

(f) co-operate with the requests of any Approved Divestiture Agent or Approved Independent 
Auditor appointed pursuant to this Undertaking; 
(g) provide the following reports directly to the ACCC: 
(i) 
a monthly written report regarding the implementation of and any suggested 
changes to the Approved Separation and Management Plan; and

(ii) 
an immediate report of any issues that arise in relation to the implementation of

(iii) 
providing and paying for any external expertise, assistance or advice required by

Approved Independent Manager; and 
(c) not interfere with the authority of, or otherwise hinder, the Approved Independent 
Manager’s ability to carry out his or her obligations as the Approved Independent 
Manager, including:

(i)
