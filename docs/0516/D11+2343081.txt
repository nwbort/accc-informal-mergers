1 Persons giving this Variation
1.1 This Variation is given to the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC)
by:
(a) lnvoCare Limited (ACN 096 437 393) (lnvoGare), on behalf of itself and its

2 Background
2.1 On 8 June 2011, the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACGC)
accepted an undertaking (the Undertaking) under section 878 of the Competition and
Consumer Act 2010 (the Act) from lnvoCare in connection with the Proposed
Acquisition.
2.2 lnvoCare has applied to the ACCC to vary the Undertaking with the purpose of reducing
the period in which lnvoCare is restricted from utilising the services of the Approved
lndependent Manager for a purpose other than compliance with the Undertaking.
3 Commencement
3.1 This Variation comes into effect when:
(a) this Variation is executed by lnvoCare; and
(b) the Commission accepts this Variation so executed.
4 Variation to clause 8.3 - Obligations in relation to the Approved
lndependent Manager
4.1 Clause 8.3(c)(vi) of the Undertaking is amended by deleting the words "12 months" and
inserting the words "3 months" in their place.
4.2 The varied Clause 8.3(c)(vi) of the Undertaking will accordingly read as follows:
Not appoint the Approved lndependent Manager, or have any agreements,
understandings or arrangements with the Approved lndependent Manager, to utilise the
Approved lndependent Manager's services for anything other than compliance with the
Undertaking until at least 3 months after the Approved lndependent Manager ceases to
act in the role of the Approved lndependent Manager.
5 Defined terms and interpretat¡on
5.'1 Any term defined in the Undertaking and not defined in this Variation has the meaning
given in the Undertaking, unless the contrary intention appears.
5.2 Variation means this variation to the Undertaking.
