2. 
Background
The Parties to the Proposed Acquisition and the transaction details
2.I 
Acquirer: Stradbroke Assets (a wholly owned subsidiary of Transit
Systems Ferries). Transit Systems Ferries is part of a privately owned
group of companies which includes Transit Systems. Transit is involved in
the operation of a number of transport operations. These transport
operations include a scheduled vehicle ferry service on the Brisbane-North
Stradbroke Island route and scheduled passenger services to the south
Moreton Bay islands.
2.2 Target: Stradbroke Ferries. The privately owned Stradbroke Ferries is
involved in the operation of a number of ferry services in Moreton Bay,
Queensland. These ferry services include a scheduled vehicle ferry service
on the Brisbane-North Stradbroke Island Route and scheduled vehicle
ferry services to the south Moreton Bay islands.
The Proposed Acquisition
2.3 On I April 2011 Stradbroke Assets made offers to acquire all of the issued
shares in Stradbroke Ferries.
2.4 Completion of the proposed acquisition is subject to Transit Systems
Ferries obtaining written confirmation from the ACCC that it has no
objection to the transaction for the purposes of section 50 of the Act.
The ACCC's review
2.5 On 5 April 201I, the ACCC commenced its public review of the Proposed
Acquisition.

2.6 The ACCC undertook market inquiries and considered information
provided by the parties, industry participants and others. The ACCC's
inquiries were aimed at assessing whether or not the Proposed Acquisition
would have the effect, or be likely to have the effect, of substantially
lessening competition in a market in contravention of section 50 of the
Act.
The ACCC's competition concerns
2.6 The ACCC considered that, in the absence of the Undertaking, the
Proposed Acquisition would result, or be likely to result, in a substantial
lessening of competition in relation to the market for vehicle ferry services
on the Brisbane-North Stradbroke Island Route.
2.7 The ACCC considered that the Proposed Acquisition would remove
Stradbroke Ferries as a vigorous and effective competitor to Transit and
leave Transit as the only supplier of vehicle ferry services on the
Brisbane-North Stradbroke Island Route.
2.8 The ACCC considered that there are very high barriers to entry for the
supply of vehicle ferry services on the Brisbane-North Stradbroke Island
Route and that the threat of new entry would not be likely to constrain the
merged firm. These barriers to entry largely consist of sunk capital costs
and environmental regulatory restrictions in developing new landing sites,
as well as the presence of exclusive access rights over existing landing
sites.
2.9 The ACCC noted that the threat of switching by customers was unlikely to
constrain the merged firm.
2.10 The ACCC considered markets relating to the south Moreton Bay islands
but did not express concerns in relation to those or any other markets.
The proposed Undertaking remedy
2.ll V/hile Transit does not agree with the concerns expressed by the ACCC,
Transit has entered into the Surrender Agreement and given this
Undertaking to address the ACCC's concerns in a timely manner.
2.12 The objective of the Undertaking is to address the ACCC's competition
concerns that would otherwise arise as a consequence of the Proposed
Acquisition.

potential new entrant on the Brisbane-North Stradbroke Island Route has
access to landing sites by:
