Re:  Shareholding disclosure -  proposed acquisition of Asciano Limited 
As you are aware, the ACCC is currently reviewing the proposed acquisition of the shares and 
assets of Asciano Limited (Asciano) by various members of a consortium comprising Brookfield 
Infrastructure Partners L.P. (and certain of its affiliates) (Brookfield), Qube Holdings Limited 
(Qube), Global Infrastructure Management, LLC (on behalf of itself and its managed funds and 
clients) (GIP), Canada Pension Plan Investment Board (CPPIB), CIC Capital Corporation (CIC 
Capital), British Columbia Investment Management Corporation (bcIMC), GIC Private Limited 
(GIC) and Qatar Investment Authority (QIA) (collectively, the Consortium) (together, the 
proposed acquisition). 
Mr Mick Keogh, who has recently been appointed a Commissioner of the ACCC, has declared to 
the Chairman of the ACCC that he holds a shareholding in Qube Limited valued at approximately 
$7,800.  
After consideration by the ACCC Chairman and Commissioners excluding Mr Keogh, the 
Commissioners have concluded that the shareholding does not amount to a material personal 
interest for the purposes of section 29 of the Public Governance, Performance and Accountability 
Act 2015 (Cth), nor a pecuniary interest which could conflict with the proper performance of Mr 
Keogh’s functions in relation to the ACCC’s consideration of the proposed acquisition for the 
purposes of section 17 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth).   
In these circumstances, it has been determined by the Chairman and Commissioners that Mr 
Keogh is able to take part in the ACCC’s consideration of the proposed acquisition.  Accordingly, 
Mr Keogh will now take part in the ACCC’s consideration of the proposed acquisition.
